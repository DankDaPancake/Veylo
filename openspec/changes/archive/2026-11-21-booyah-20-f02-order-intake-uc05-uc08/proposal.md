# Proposal

## Why

PA3 Section D requires reviewable specifications and screen evidence for all four F02 use cases. The clean remake gives reviewers one traceable package for walking the flows and comparing the proposed Order Intake contract with the Section E work.

## What Changes

- **In scope** — Produce English Section D Markdown, an A4 PDF, a repository working copy of Công Khánh's original PrototypeKit with fresh desktop state screens and PNGs, and synthetic CSV fixtures for UC05–UC08. Tie each flow step to its requirement, rule and visible state.
- Reconcile FR-02-1–5 and the cross-cutting FR-01-2 with BOOYAH-24 and BOOYAH-26. Record concrete D-F02-1–8 values, examples, sources and review status. Treat the current Jira status as issue metadata and record acceptance of the remake separately.
- Check the CSV and state contract against authorized BOOYAH-13 and BOOYAH-19 evidence and hand the complete machine-verified package to Phạm Công Khánh and the team for review.
- **Out of scope** — Production UI, APIs, persistence, schema and migrations, later Order and DeliveryAttempt workflows, changes to BOOYAH-13 artifacts, publication during planning and modifications to source drafts. Use only the clean-exploration inputs and Công Khánh's original kit for prototype work.
