# Veylo — F04/F08 Business Rules Proposal

Performed by: Trần Lê Hải. Reviewed by: Nguyễn Thành Trung (pending actual review). Edited by: Codex (AI-assisted drafting); human editor pending.


| Rule Name                                                       | Proposed Content                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **D-F04-1 — Role, hub and assignment authorization**           | UC11/UC12 require an active Rider and their own published assignment in their authorized hub; recheck on every read, event and POD request. UC13/UC19 require a same-hub Dispatcher. Administrator has no implicit Dispatcher permission. Derive identity/hub from the trusted session. Proposed responses align with F02:`401` for absent/expired authentication, `403` for a denied role/inactive account, and `404` for inaccessible resource identifiers. Guide access does not grant order access. **Pending:** response-policy confirmation with F01/F02 owners.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **D-F04-2 — Published work and automatic previews**            | On opening a published shift, UC11 displays the ordered assigned work and automatically requests the next-stop suggestion and the published guide preview for the relevant selected stop. The default selected stop is the active attempt's stop, otherwise the suggested stop, otherwise the first eligible stop in published order. Either preview may be absent without blocking the list or manual stop selection. EP11-A opens UC14's explanation; EP11-B opens UC15's full guide. Only same-hub published guides may appear; exclude raw notes, drafts, recipient details, access codes, order records and POD photos from shared guides. UC15 remains independently searchable off shift.**Pending:** owner walkthrough; automatic previews and guide audience are established in Vision 2.2-review/UCM 0.4. |
| **D-F04-3 — Start eligibility and sequential execution**       | Start only currently assigned, published, unresolved work with no active attempt, successful outcome, terminal unsuccessful resolution or unresolved failed-attempt decision. Exclude`CANCELLED` orders and completed shifts. Proposed: future published work is viewable but cannot start before the Shift's planned start; unfinished published work after its planned end remains eligible with an overdue warning. Allow one active attempt per Rider and per Order; resume it before another stop. A failed stop needs UC13 and a new opportunity. After each acknowledged outcome, refresh UC11 and continue eligible assigned work. Choosing another stop does not reorder the Shift. **Pending:** time/sequential/concurrency rules with F03/F05.                                                           |
| **D-F04-4 — Attempt events and evidence**                      | Keep each DeliveryAttempt linked to its Order, executing Rider, originating Shift/assignment and authoritative event times. Observable milestones are started, arrived and an immutable successful/failed outcome; these are domain terms, not new database enums. Use server-acknowledged start, arrival and outcome-commit instants; display them in`Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`. Success requires recorded arrival; failure may occur before arrival with a truthful reason/comment and no invented arrival time. Finished attempts are never overwritten by retries. **Pending:** early-failure policy and final state spelling.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **D-F04-5 — Success and photographic POD**                     | Successful completion requires one uploaded, validated, privately accessible photo bound to the current attempt. Proposed formats: JPEG or PNG, decoded as an image, at most**5,000,000 bytes**; reject empty, corrupt, oversized or unsupported files. Allow replacement before confirmation. Show success only after POD linkage, attempt outcome and the Order's delivered business outcome commit together. Upload failure leaves the attempt active and permits retry; never substitute a failure outcome automatically. No signature, GPS proof or recipient identity capture is added. **Pending:** photo constraints and delivered-outcome representation.                                                                                                                                                  |
| **D-F04-6 — Failed attempt and structured reasons**            | A failed attempt requires one reason from the proposed set**RECIPIENT_UNREACHABLE, RECIPIENT_UNAVAILABLE, INCORRECT_ADDRESS, ACCESS_BLOCKED, OTHER**, plus a trimmed **1–500-character** factual comment; OTHER must explain the obstacle. Do not require a photo for failure or invent location facts. Failure closes only that attempt and places the Order in the derived “awaiting dispatcher resolution” queue; it does not set a terminal Order failure, restore start eligibility or authorize retry. **Pending:** reason set and comment limit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **D-F04-7 — Duplicate submission, connectivity and atomicity** | Proposed: start, arrival, outcome and UC13 decisions use a stable logical-operation identifier and server-side replay protection. Identical replays return the original result; incompatible replays or stale versions are rejected. Disable duplicate taps while pending, but do not rely on UI controls alone. On an unknown network result, reconcile authoritative state before resending or advancing; do not create another attempt. No offline mutation queue is promised. An upload alone is not a completion.**Pending:** exact API/idempotency design; no schema change is made here.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **D-F04-8 — Leaving before outcome**                           | Dismissing an outcome form does not cancel an Order or invent an attempt outcome. Before start acknowledgement, reconcile an uncertain start first; if no start exists, return without an attempt. After acknowledged start/arrival, retain the active attempt and offer Resume; other starts remain blocked. Unsaved comments/photo selection remain only while the authorized view stays open; warn that closing/reloading may require re-entry/reselection. Do not introduce an ABORTED state or silently unlock abandoned work.**Pending:** exceptional operational recovery for a permanently abandoned attempt.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **D-F04-9 — Monitoring and resolution timing**                 | UC13 may monitor recorded progress at any time, with last-refreshed time and manual refresh; no continuous GPS is implied. Following Vision workflow 1, allow failed-order resolution only when the originating published shift has no active attempt and no remaining executable assigned stop. Until then show Awaiting remaining stops and retain the failed attempt. Resolve each unresolved latest failure once; successful orders bypass this decision. “No stops remain” alone does not declare the shift completed.**Pending:** explicit F03 shift-completion integration.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **D-F04-10 — Retry eligibility and handoff**                   | Proposed maximum:**three completed failed attempts per Order across all shifts**, including the initial attempt. Allow re-dispatch only while the count is below three, the latest failure is unresolved, and no successful outcome, active attempt, cancellation, terminal resolution or other live assignment exists. UC13 authorizes one new delivery opportunity and retires the failed assignment from executable work; UC09/UC10 subsequently apply their rider/capacity/publication checks. No attempt is created by this decision. Dismissing the planning handoff preserves the recorded authorization and leaves the Order awaiting planning; re-entry reuses it. **Pending:** cap, one-opportunity semantics and compatibility with F03's NEW-only pool.                                                 |
| **D-F04-11 — Terminal resolution and separate lifecycles**     | UC13 may explicitly close an unresolved failed Order as**terminal unsuccessful**, with a trimmed **5–300-character** reason and confirmation, either below the cap when no further delivery is appropriate or at the cap when retry is blocked. The cap never closes the Order automatically. Delivered and terminal unsuccessful are proposed business outcomes, with no reopening in these UCs; final persisted names remain a shared review item. Preserve F02's `NEW`/`CANCELLED` intake slice and its no-reversal rule. No new Order enum or transition is applied. **Pending:** joint F02/F03/F04/OpenSpec lifecycle agreement.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **D-F04-12 — Concurrent changes and F02 compatibility**        | Recheck current assignment, attempt, Order and shift eligibility with the same atomic operation that starts/completes an attempt or resolves an Order. UC10 cannot move active work; a race rejects the losing operation and offers refresh. Preserve history and reject stale/duplicate resolutions. F03 currently keeps assigned Orders`NEW`, whereas UC08 checks only `NEW` to edit/cancel: **propose a future shared guard preventing ordinary F02 edits/cancellation while assigned or after an attempt exists**. This guard is a review proposal, not an edit to F02/OpenSpec; dependent implementation must await reconciliation.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **D-F08-1 — Report scope and time**                            | Proposed: filter by inclusive**Shift start dates** in `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`, optional Shift and Rider, all within the trusted hub; combine filters with AND. Default to today, all permitted shifts/riders. Require valid calendar dates and start ≤ end; no arbitrary maximum range is introduced. Use one visible “as of” snapshot. Only published/completed work is eligible. This is a shift-cohort report, not a count of all events occurring during the selected dates; show that distinction. **Pending:** filter/date-cohort agreement.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **D-F08-2 — Order and attempt metrics**                        | Count each eligible Order once in the selected work, even across selected retry shifts; count each distinct completed attempt separately. Order delivery rate = distinct Orders with a successful attempt in the selected work / distinct eligible Orders in that work × 100. Attempt success rate = successful completed attempts / all completed attempts × 100. Include eligible unattempted Orders in the Order denominator; show active attempts separately and exclude them from attempt-rate denominators. Recompute grand totals from underlying IDs, never average percentages or sum distinct-Order subtotals. Companion UC19 defines removal, cancellation, retry attribution and zero denominators.**Pending:** all formulas/cohort rules.                                                            |
| **D-F08-3 — Duration and failure causes**                      | Average attempt duration = arithmetic mean of outcome-commit time minus acknowledged start time, in**minutes**, for completed in-scope attempts with valid start ≤ arrival ≤ finish, or start ≤ finish for a permitted pre-arrival failure. Include elapsed travel, waiting and upload time; do not call this rider working time. Missing/negative/invalid timestamps are excluded from duration only and shown as a quality-warning count. Group completed failed attempts by recorded reason; failure share uses all completed failed attempts, including an Unknown legacy bucket. **Pending:** measurement boundary and aggregation definitions.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **D-F08-4 — Empty, partial and stale reports**                 | Display zero counts and**N/A** for undefined rates/means; never turn incomplete attempts into failed outcomes or zero-duration rows. Separate valid empty results from load failure. Label active/pending-resolution counts and data-quality exclusions; preserve the last valid filters/report when a refresh fails, with a stale warning. Access revocation clears protected data. Reports are read-only and refreshed from recorded events; no export, GPS stream, billing, payroll or automatic terminal decision is added. **Pending:** report presentation/quality-policy review.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
