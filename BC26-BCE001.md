# Next-Best Delivery Proposal

Source: [BC26-BCE001.pdf](BC26-BCE001.pdf) — a one-page infographic displaying the Shopee brand.

## Core Bottleneck

Riders’ ability to make effective delivery decisions varies with experience and accumulated local knowledge.

## Mechanism

### Pillar 1: Rider Productivity Gap

| Rider group | Orders/day |
| --- | ---: |
| New | 45 |
| Tenured | 270 |

Observed gap: **37 pp FTD** and **6× orders/day**.

### Pillar 2: Multi-Variable Decision Trap

Delivery decisions involve five variables: **WHO, WHERE, WHEN, HOW, SUCCESS**.

**Distance alone ≠ delivery success.**

### Pillar 3: Cost of Failed Attempts

- New riders: **40% FTD failure = ~18 failed attempts/day**.
- Failed attempts consume travel, waiting, and contact time.
- Poor choices waste rider time.

## Decision Point

**What is the next best delivery to maximize rider productivity and delivery success?**

| Step | Finding |

## Solution

**Input → AI Engine → Next-Best Delivery → Actions → Rider Executes → Output**

### Input

- **Order:** SLA, time window, parcel type, status.
- **Customer:** history, response signals, COD readiness.
- **Location:** GPS, building/road, access history.
- **Rider delivery process:** GPS traces, access points, parking, security, rider feedback.
- **Real-time context:** traffic, temporary access, weather.

### AI Engine

Three things AI predicts:

1. **P(Success):** Probability of completing the delivery now.
2. **True Delivery Time:** Travel + Access + Waiting + Handover time.
3. **Priority:** When is the delivery due? What is the best route?

### Decide: Next-Best Delivery

Select the order with the highest expected successful-delivery value/unit of rider time.

### Actions

1. **Route Optimization**

   SLA + time window + P(success) + friction + access + rider capacity.

2. **Micro-guide**

   > “Gửi xe tại cổng B, đi thang máy bảo vệ vì thang khách đang bảo trì.”

3. **Customer Handshake**

   > “Đơn hàng của bạn sẽ tới trong 2p nữa! Hãy xuống nhận ngay bây giờ để bác tài giao đơn tiếp theo nhé!”

### Rider Executes

Follow guidance; the rider can override it if real-world barriers arise.

### Output

**Delivery Success ↔ Collective Delivery Memory**

GPS + feedback + outcomes → better future predictions.

### Continuous Learning Loop

Output feeds back into Input through the continuous learning loop.

## Why It Works?

| Existing gap | Intervention | Bottleneck relief |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Travel route ≠ true delivery effort | True Delivery Time | ↓ Access & waiting (Reed et al., 2024) |
| Availability not systematically integrated | Predictive Sequencing + Handshake | ↑ First-Attempt Success (Lim et al., 2023; Voigt et al., 2023) |
| Rider knowledge remains fragmented | Collective Delivery Memory | Faster new-rider learning |
| Order selection is not context-aware | Next-Best Delivery | ↑ Successful deliveries/rider-hour (Lim et al., 2023; Voigt et al., 2023) |
